## Unit 6 — Colours（颜色）

### A · 核心句型 / 对话

- Look at the kite!
- What colour is it?
- It's red, white, yellow, green, blue and black.

### B · 儿歌 / Chant

- What colour is the kite?
- What colours can you see?
- Red, red, red.
- It's my favourite colour.
- What colour is the kite?
- What colours can you see?
- Yellow, yellow, yellow.
- It's my favourite colour.
- What colour is the kite?
- What colours can you see?
- Green, green, green.
- It's my favourite colour.

### C · 词汇 / 巩固

- This is a pencil. It's yellow.

- This is a ruler. It's blue.
